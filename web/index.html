How to read this

Margins of error

Every figure is a survey estimate, not a count. The margin of error (MOE) is the 90 percent margin published by the Census Bureau, combined with the standard formulas when an indicator is a sum or a ratio of several table cells. A value of 20 percent with an MOE of 5 points means the true value is likely between 15 and 25 percent. Small tracts and rare conditions have wide margins.

Reliability is based on the coefficient of variation (CV), the standard error divided by the estimate: Low reliability tracts are drawn with diagonal hatching on the map and flagged in the table. Do not rank tracts whose margins overlap.
